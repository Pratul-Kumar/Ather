# Prompts

The following prompts were used to generate the source video assets for the Project Aether cinematic trailer. They are designed for modern generative video AI tools (such as Runway Gen-2, Sora, or Pika).

### Generation Settings (General)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Motion:** Low / Slow (to ensure smooth, cinematic movement)
- **Negative Prompt:** Text, watermarks, fast motion, bright colors, cartoon, low resolution, 3d render.

---

### Shot 1
**Concept:** The Awakening
> **Prompt:** Extreme close-up of a single human eye slowly opening, the iris reflecting a vast starfield. Ultra-slow motion. The iris glows faintly with a cool ice-blue bioluminescence. Background is pitch black. Cinematic lighting, anamorphic lens flare, photorealistic, 4K, eerie, atmospheric sci-fi.

### Shot 2
**Concept:** The Signal
> **Prompt:** Aerial bird's-eye view of a massive, perfectly geometric structure emerging from dense fog above a dark ocean. The structure pulses with faint amber light. Clouds swirl slowly around it. Silence implied, cinematic wide shot, moody overcast sky, muted palette, high-concept sci-fi, photorealistic.

### Shot 3
**Concept:** The Departure
> **Prompt:** A lone silhouetted figure standing on the edge of a vast dark cliffside, looking up at a towering alien monolith floating in the sky, surrounded by swirling aurora light. Godray volumetric lighting, cinematic wide shot, deep moody blues and silvers. Slow camera push forward. Photorealistic, awe-inspiring scale.
