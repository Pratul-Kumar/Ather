# Prompts

The following prompts were used to generate the source video assets for the Project Aether cinematic trailer. They are designed for modern generative video AI tools (such as Runway Gen-2, Sora, or Pika).

### Generation Settings (General)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Motion:** Low / Slow (to ensure smooth, cinematic movement)
- **Negative Prompt:** Text, watermarks, fast motion, bright colors, cartoon, low resolution, 3d render.

---

### Shot 1
**Concept:** The Awakening
> **Prompt:** 
A breathtaking cinematic ultra-wide establishing shot of a vast, desolate alien landscape at dawn. Thick volumetric fog slowly rolls across the ground, partially revealing an enormous ancient monolithic structure standing alone in the distance. A lone human figure, barely visible, stands in the foreground facing the mysterious monument, emphasizing its immense scale.

The environment is dominated by deep charcoal and slate tones (#1A1D20), with only a faint warm muted-gold glow (#D4C5A9) escaping from a narrow crack within the monolith. The atmosphere feels quiet, eerie, and filled with anticipation. Soft drifting dust particles, subtle cinematic haze, realistic lighting, and gentle film grain create a premium feature-film look.

Camera movement: an extremely slow cinematic dolly push toward the monolith with smooth, stable motion. No sudden movement, no camera shake.

Style: high-concept science-fiction mystery, photorealistic, atmospheric, minimalist, cinematic, anamorphic composition, shallow contrast, realistic textures, volumetric lighting, epic scale.

**Negative Prompt:** No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no neon colors, no cyberpunk city, no spaceships, no explosions, no fantasy creatures, no oversaturated colors, no fast motion, no low-quality artifacts, no cartoon style.

### Shot 2
**Concept:** The Signal
> **Prompt:**
Continue from the previous scene. The camera slowly approaches the ancient monolithic structure through dense rolling fog. The massive black stone surface is weathered with precise geometric carvings that hint at advanced extraterrestrial engineering. As the camera moves closer, thin glowing lines begin to appear across the surface, forming intricate patterns. A subtle warm muted-gold light (#D4C5A9) slowly spreads through the carvings, as though the structure is awakening in response to an unseen signal.

The atmosphere is silent and tense. Floating dust particles drift through the air, while soft volumetric fog wraps around the monument. The environment remains dominated by deep charcoal and slate tones (#1A1D20), with restrained ice-white (#F5F5F7) highlights on the edges of the stone.

Camera movement: slow, smooth cinematic push-in with a slight orbit around one corner of the monolith, maintaining steady, film-like motion.

Style: photorealistic, premium science-fiction mystery, atmospheric, minimalist, feature-film cinematography, anamorphic lens, realistic lighting, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain.

**Negative Prompt:** No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no cyberpunk city, no neon blue or purple lighting, no spaceships, no explosions, no fantasy elements, no fast camera movement, no cartoon style, no oversaturated colors.

### Shot 3
**Concept:** The Departure
> **Prompt:**
Continue seamlessly from the previous shot. The camera is now very close to the ancient extraterrestrial mechanism embedded within the monolith. Intricate concentric rings and geometric channels carved into the dark surface begin to awaken. A restrained warm muted-gold energy (#D4C5A9) slowly travels through the channels, illuminating the mechanism with precision. Dust particles float through the air as the atmosphere remains silent and tense.

As the camera performs a slow cinematic push toward the center of the mechanism, the glowing energy converges into a single circular core. In the final second, the core emits one controlled pulse of brilliant ice-white light (#F5F5F7), briefly illuminating the surrounding stone before fading back into darkness, leaving a perfect cinematic pause for the title reveal.

The environment remains dominated by deep charcoal and slate (#1A1D20), with subtle volumetric fog, realistic reflections, shallow depth of field, premium film grain, and feature-film quality lighting. The overall feeling is mysterious, ancient, intelligent, and unsettling rather than action-heavy.

Camera movement: extremely slow macro push-in with smooth, stable motion. No camera shake.

Style: photorealistic, high-end science-fiction mystery, cinematic, atmospheric, minimalist, anamorphic lens, realistic materials, premium VFX quality.

**Negative Prompt:** No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no explosions, no lightning effects, no colorful neon, no cyberpunk city, no spaceships, no fantasy creatures, no excessive particles, no cartoon style, no oversaturated colors, no fast motion.
